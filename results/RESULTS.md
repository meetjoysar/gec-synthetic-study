## Metric notes

- **ERRANT reports P = 1.0 for the copy baseline.** ERRANT defines `p = tp/(tp+fp) if fp else 1.0`, so a system that proposes no edits at all has undefined precision reported as 1.0. F0.5 = 0 is the meaningful number there.
- **Two BLEU columns.** `BLEU(13a)` is computed on detokenised text with sacreBLEU's default tokenisation and is the number comparable with published GEC work; `BLEU(tok)` is computed on our space-separated tokenisation with `tokenize="none"`. They differ by several points.
- **Over-correction rate** is the share of already-correct test sentences (gold target equals source) that the model nevertheless changed.
